# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a tool for print shops, and the community around it should feel like a
good shop floor: direct, practical, and nobody made to feel stupid for asking.

## Expected

- **Assume competence.** Someone who has run a press for twenty years and has never opened a
  terminal is not a beginner. They know things you don't.
- **Be direct about code, generous about people.** "This will double-charge 2XL" is good review.
  "Did you even test this?" is not.
- **Explain the why.** Especially around pricing and costing, where the reasoning is the valuable
  part.
- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements here are two people with different shops, both right
  about their own.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information
- Deliberately derailing discussions or wearing people down through persistence
- Posting real customer data — names, emails, order details — in issues or PRs. Scrub it.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ColeLundstrom) or by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/ColeLundstrom/printshopcrm/security/advisories/new),
which is a private channel to the maintainer whether or not the issue is security-related.

Reports are handled confidentially. Responses run from a private word to a permanent ban, depending
on severity. If the person you need to report is the maintainer, say so publicly — a project where
that can't be raised isn't worth contributing to.
