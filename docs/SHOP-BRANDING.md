# Make the workspace yours

Open **Setup & connections → Make it yours · logo & colors**, or **Settings → Logo & colors**. This is optional and available to every shop without an AI connection or paid feature tier.

- Upload a shop logo (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF or SVG, up to 5 MB). The logo appears in the workspace header and on the existing customer document views. Logo uploads/removal save immediately; rejected uploads preserve the previous logo.
- Name your workspace and add a tagline. The legal shop name remains a separate setting so changing the app title does not silently rename invoices.
- Choose primary and secondary colors by picker or six-digit hex value. Preview both light and dark appearances before saving. Shades adapt for readable text and controls; status/error colors keep their meanings. Customer PDFs retain their document styling.
- Choose the shop's default appearance: light, dark, device preference, or the app default. Each device can still toggle its own appearance. Per-shop device preferences do not cross to a different shop.
- **Reset colors** previews the defaults. Save to apply; this retains the logo and workspace name.

Owners and managers can save branding. Staff can choose their own light/dark appearance. Changes are stored in the shop database and existing live tabs refresh their branding without replacing unsaved forms. Another shop cannot read the uploaded logo or inherit the colors.

The source/license links remain available. Self-hosters can also fork and modify the AGPL source under its license terms; choosing a theme does not require maintaining a fork.
