## Branch-only slice: payment terms

Before: the customer card carried only a free-text `net-30` tag or note, and converting an approved quote showed a date field prefilled 14 calendar days ahead. The server used a separate 15-day fallback if a caller omitted that date. A repeat order required retyping or checking the due date and the two defaults could disagree. After: the customer has a structured Due on receipt / Net 15 / Net 30 / Net 45 default. Conversion shows that choice, accepts a one-invoice date override, and computes the due date on the server from invoice creation. Production due remains independent. In the local synthetic browser review, a Net 30 customer converted a repeat quote once and the resulting invoice showed November 10 from October 11; revisiting the quote displayed its invoice link and no Convert button. The focused HTTP test also checks unauthenticated edits, invalid term rejection, explicit override, duplicate conversion and unchanged earlier invoice due date.

The added application code in `lib/payment-terms.mjs`, `lib/db.mjs`, `server.mjs` and two existing views was written for PrintShopCRM from these observed workflow requirements and the project's own conventions.
