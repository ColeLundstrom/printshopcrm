# Job calendar and optional workflow timing

Open Calendar from the card board or a production job. The card board remains available. Month and week grids switch to a readable agenda on small screens; Agenda is also available on desktop. Entries show customer due dates, planned production, and pending task deadlines. Department and “Assigned to me” filters narrow the view. Undated jobs appear under Unscheduled jobs.

In Edit workflows, enable the optional timeline and choose normal days from start to production. Use working days (Monday–Friday) or calendar days. Working days skip weekends, not holidays. A task's negative offset is before production, zero is production day, and positive is after production. Blank offsets leave tasks untimed. Tasks use the same day basis as their job.

New jobs with automatic workflows snapshot these rules at creation. Applying a template manually snapshots it too. The planning start defaults to the job's recorded creation date. Template changes affect future jobs only. Combined templates retain one shared task per matching title/department/stage/requirement/employee and take its earliest offset; the enabled template with the longest turnaround supplies the combined job's day basis and duration. Save a combined workflow when a recurring combination needs different rules.

Open a job → Edit timeline to override its start, duration, day basis or production date. A production-date override moves relative task targets. Edit a pending task to pin a specific date; pinned dates remain fixed until cleared. A schedule note is retained in task history, and concurrent stale edits return a conflict. A production date after the customer due date displays a warning; it does not rewrite the customer promise.

Timing is advisory. Turning it off hides planned production/task dates while preserving saved settings, task order and assignment. Future dates never prevent completing work early. Adding a timeline to a job without a task workflow retains normal card-board and artwork-stage controls. The customer due date remains independently editable on the full job.

Managers edit dates; staff view calendar and deadlines with existing shop permissions. Department filters are views, not new access-control boundaries. Completed task targets disappear from the calendar; completion history remains on the job. The calendar lists active jobs, supports up to 63 days per request, and clearly warns when its 5,000-entry display limit is reached. Unscheduled previews show 100 jobs with a total count.

This is job planning, not capacity optimization or a resource-booking engine. Holidays, machine conflict detection, drag-to-reschedule and external calendar synchronization are not implemented. Actual work ordering continues through the existing task gates.
